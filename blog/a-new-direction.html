A New Direction

Why I'm discontinuing my previous projects and shifting toward theoretical work.

October 23, 2026 · Pietro Abruzzo(Founder of DuckSoft)

As you may have noticed, I have temporarily paused my development activities to prioritize my educational objectives. During this period, I have completed several data science courses and commenced my studies in mathematics at the University of Bielefeld.

After careful consideration, I have decided to discontinue my previous projects in order to focus on new initiatives. Moving forward, my attention will shift toward theoretical projects rather than business-oriented ones such as Duck AC or the Business Generator. This transition reflects my longstanding interest in theoretical machine learning and mathematics. Additionally, my engagement with LLMs and agents has diminished over time.

Currently, I am working on research related to the generalization problem in mathematics, which is expected to require significant time to complete. Concurrently, I am developing educational software aimed at enhancing high school and university students' comprehension of mathematical concepts through visual learning. I will provide further information on this project in due course.

Please feel free to reach out if you have specific requests, which I may consider. I appreciate your understanding regarding this shift in focus and look forward to embarking on this new phase of my professional journey.
